## Implementation and decisions

Queries expose distinct loading, empty success and rejected states with an explicit retry action. A persistent focusable status receives focus when retry starts; completion updates it without moving focus again. Responses from superseded queries are ignored. Results are displayed only when their returned area and unavailable filter match the accepted selection, preventing old listings and distances from appearing in a new area. The previous snapshot remains saved in renderer state; same-query refresh failures preserve selected details, availability and successful-check timestamps.

Coverage status, last successful covering check and caveats come from the result envelope. Sample labeling and existing location uncertainty explanations remain visible. Tile-provider failures leave the list, details and coordinate controls usable. No new dependencies, collection requests, geocoding, backend functionality or source activation were introduced. No approved deviations.
