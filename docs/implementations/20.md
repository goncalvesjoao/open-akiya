## Implementation and decisions

Ordinary imports reuse one successful geographically covering scope, never inferred unions or mere overlap. Cached requests retain their requested area and refer to the covering succeeded job. Queued requests re-evaluate reuse before transport. Recent successful offer observations are reused independently across area overlaps, with private source/filter/reference fingerprints and unchanged successful-check timestamps. The configurable area and detail defaults are 24 hours; exact expiry is stale. Area expiry is capped by the oldest required detail check, including changed policy after restart and recovered legacy progress.

Enabled-source set, adapter/query capabilities, geographic-reference version and fixed filters identify semantics. Show unavailable remains presentation-only. Saved results expose covering successful evidence and persistent limitation/failure caveats. Incomplete or failed work cannot renew successful evidence. Source execution recovers persisted source identity rather than assuming configured order. No approved deviations.

Storage/reimport/executor regressions explicitly configure zero freshness when their purpose requires forced checks. Existing multi-source tests lacked deterministic pacing waits and timed out on the unmodified base; they now use the shared controlled clock. The all-rejected outcome expectation follows #5's failed result without useful progress. No new dependency, DTO, renderer or live-source collection is introduced.
